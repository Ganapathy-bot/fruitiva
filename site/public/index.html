Selected formulation

Mango Toffee

Ingredients

MANGO
  • Mango pulp1 kg
  • Sugar600 g
  • Glucose100 g
  • Skimmed milk powder160 g
  • Butter or hydrogenated fat100 g

Manufacturing workflow

01

Wash, peel and pulp ripe mangoes.

02

Concentrate the pulp to one-third of its original volume.

03

Add the remaining ingredients and mix thoroughly.

04

Continue cooking until the desired consistency is reached.

05

Spread into a 0.5–0.75 cm sheet on a lightly greased surface.

06

Allow to cool and set.

07

Cut into individual toffee pieces.

08

Wrap and pack in airtight containers.

📏

Target sheet thickness

0.5–0.75 cm

Banana Fruit Bar

FORMULATION
  • Banana pulp1 kg
  • Sugar100 g
  • Citric acid2 g
  • KMS1 g

Banana fruit bar process

01

Fruit selection and washing.

02

Peeling and pulping.

03

Heating at 83 °C for 10 minutes.

04

Mixing with sugar, citric acid and KMS.

05

Spreading the pulp uniformly on drying trays.

06

Drying in a cross-flow dryer at 60 °C until a removable sheet forms.

07

Cutting the dried sheet into small bars.

08

Wrapping and packing in pouches.

Recipe 01

Aonla Laddu & Burfi

Ingredients

  • Aonla shred1 kg
  • Sugar1 kg
  • Water0.5 litre
  • Melon seeds100 g
Preparation process ＋
Wash → Grate → Dry or squeeze lightly → Add to sugar syrup → Cook to the desired consistency → Add melon seeds → Shape into laddus or burfi → Dry lightly in shade → Pack.

Recipe 02

Papaya Burfi

Ingredients

  • Papaya1 kg
  • Sugar750 g
  • Cardamom powder10 g
  • Coconut shreds20 g
Preparation process ＋
Wash → Peel → Remove seeds → Grate → Add sugar → Cook with stirring → Add cardamom and coconut → Spread on a greased tray → Set → Garnish with coconut → Cut into pieces → Pack.

Recipe 03

Carrot Burfi

Ingredients

  • Carrot1 kg
  • Sugar450 g
  • Milk0.5 litre
  • Ghee200 g
  • Cashew nuts and raisins50 g
  • Cardamom1 g
Preparation process ＋
Scrape → Wash → Grate → Cook with milk until the milk dries → Fry in ghee → Add sugar, dry fruits and cardamom → Spread in a greased tray → Allow to set → Cut into burfi pieces → Pack.
