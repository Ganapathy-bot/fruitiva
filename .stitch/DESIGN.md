# Design System: FRUITIVA
**Project ID:** 18187089592972698893

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Premium food-tech for practical fruit processing education. The interface is dark, clean, tactile, and confident, balancing rich food photography with a clear scientific information hierarchy. Keep mobile screens polished and approachable, never textbook-like.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- **Midnight Botanical** (#101A16) – Main screen background.
- **Deep Forest** (#174C38) – Header surfaces and secondary containers.
- **Leaf Green** (#4F8A54) – Process states and botanical accents.
- **Warm Orange** (#F4A340) – Primary actions, selected states, and highlights.
- **Papaya Coral** (#F07855) – Fruit accents and supporting indicators.
- **Warm White** (#FFF9EF) – Primary text and high-contrast surfaces.
- **Muted Sage** (#A9B8AE) – Secondary text and dividers.
- **Charcoal Green** (#25332D) – Text on light surfaces.

## 3. Typography Rules
Use a highly legible modern sans-serif such as Manrope or Inter. Use strong, compact page headings, generous line height for body copy, tabular-looking ingredient quantities, and clear labels. Avoid tiny text; maintain a comfortable mobile reading size.

## 4. Component Stylings
* **Buttons:** Rounded capsules or softly rounded rectangles; warm orange for primary actions and dark/outlined variants for secondary actions.
* **Cards:** 18–24px corners, layered dark-green surfaces, subtle borders, soft shadows.
* **Inputs and tabs:** High-contrast touch targets with clear selected states.
* **Navigation:** Persistent four-item bottom navigation with icon and label; active destination uses warm orange.
* **Food photography:** Realistic, appetizing, hygienic food imagery, integrated into card layouts rather than used as a collage.

## 5. Layout Principles
Mobile-first portrait screens, safe-area-aware, vertically scrollable content, consistent horizontal padding, generous separation between sections, and no clipped recipe content. Keep headings and quantities readable at phone width.

## 6. Design System Notes for Stitch Generation

- Platform: Mobile, portrait-first
- Theme: Dark, premium food-tech, modern agricultural science education
- Background: Midnight botanical green (#101A16) with deep forest surfaces (#174C38)
- Primary Accent: Warm mango orange (#F4A340), supported by leaf green (#4F8A54) and papaya coral (#F07855)
- Text Primary: Warm white (#FFF9EF); secondary text muted sage (#A9B8AE)
